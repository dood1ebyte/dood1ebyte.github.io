Consortium Finder

Describe a project idea and get the organisations with the strongest record in the most similar EU-funded research projects (Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe, CORDIS open data), with those projects as evidence. Runs in your browser; your text is not sent anywhere.

Where EU public tenders get only one bid

Single-bid shares and typical bid counts for 27 EU countries and 10 sectors, from TED award notices. Per-tender statistics: EU Tender Radar.

Page loads, Consortium Finder searches and clicks to EU Tender Radar are counted with public hit counters (hitscounter.dev, hits.sh). Nothing you type, no cookies and no identifiers are sent; the counter services see an ordinary web request.
