    How it ranks, and its limits
    • Your text is compared with the title and objective of each EU-funded project (word-based BM25 similarity on each project's 40 most distinctive terms). The 50 most similar projects are kept.
    • Each organisation that was a coordinator or participant in those projects scores the sum of their similarity, times 1.5 when it coordinated. Third parties and associated partners are not counted.
    • Tested on 299 recently funded projects with their partners hidden, it put 22% of the real partners in its top 20, 1.9 times a ranking by activity in the same programme.
    • Only organisations that have been in EU-funded projects can appear. A record shows experience, not quality or availability: use the list as a start for contact.

    Page loads and searches are counted with public hit counters (hitscounter.dev, hits.sh). Nothing you type, no cookies and no identifiers are sent; the counter services see an ordinary web request.
